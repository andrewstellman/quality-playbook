# S5: SHIP (medium)

Role: compat. Question: who sees different output after this patch, and is it acceptable. I worked in my own copy (scratch/panel-cronstrue-list-step-separator-S5) and a base copy of HEAD~1 (scratch/panel-cronstrue-list-step-separator-S5-base). Nothing in the fixed clone was modified.

## Blind verdict (written from patch, PR.md and source before reading CONFIRMATION.md or POLICY.md)

SHIP (medium). The patch adds `i > 0` to the leading ", " strip in both branches of the multiple-values loop in `src/expressionDescriptor.ts` (about 3 changed lines). Output changes only for comma lists in day-of-month, month, day-of-week and year, where the first member is a plain value (or a step member is later in the list). I compared old and new output for these cases, and every old output I saw was visibly malformed. Behaviour change for existing users is small and acceptable. Findings below are text-level or minor.

## What I ran

- `/work/batch/runs/cronstrue/bin/run-tests <my copy>` on the fixed code: `406 passing (519ms)`.
- English, 22 expressions, old vs new (cmp.ts in my copy). Every changed line was old = glued time ("AMonly", "AMon day", "AMMonday") plus a stray " , " after "and". Examples:
  - `0 9 * * 5,*/2`: old "At 09:00 AMonly on Friday and , every 2 days of the week", new "At 09:00 AM, only on Friday and every 2 days of the week".
  - `0 9 * * 1,2,3,*/2`: new "At 09:00 AM, only on Monday, only on Tuesday, only on Wednesday, and every 2 days of the week".
  - `0 9 * * 5L,1-3/2`, `0 9 * * 5#2,1-3/2`, `0 9 L,1-5/2 * *`, `0 0 9 * * 5,*/2 2020,2022-2030/2`: old glued, new well formed.
- Unchanged by the patch (identical old and new): `0 9 * * 0-4/2,5,6`, minutes/hours lists (`5,*/10 9 * * *`, `0 5,*/2 * * *`, `0 0 5,0-30/10 * * *`), lists without a step.
- All locales (i18n build), 6 list expressions x 42 locales, old vs new. 200 output lines changed, in 38 locales (af az be bg ca cs da de en es fa fi fr he hr hu id it ko my nb nl nn pl pt_BR pt_PT ro ru sk sl sr sv sw tg th tr uk vi zh_CN zh_TW). Filter on the old lines: I looked for lines that had none of " , ", ", ,", a double space, or a glued time. Result: zero such lines. So no changed line was previously well formed. `0 9 * * 0-4/2,5,6` changed in no locale.
- ar and ja: output identical before and after (their separator is "، " / "、", so `startsWith(", ")` never matched). They stay malformed after the patch, for example ar new `" 09:00، الجمعة فقط و ، كل 2 من أيام الأسبوع, ، الأحد حتى الخميس"`. Not a regression.
- vi / sw claim in PR.md: checked `0 9 * 1,3-9/2 *` in vi. Old "Vào 09:00chỉ trong Tháng 1 và  2 tháng, ..." (double space, word lost). New "Vào 09:00, chỉ trong Tháng 1 và mỗi ngày 2 tháng, ...". The PR's own vi example (`0 0 9 * 5,*/2 * *`, 7 fields) I ran in vi: new "Vào 09:00, chỉ trong Tháng 5 và mỗi ngày 2 tháng". Matches the PR's quoted new text.
- Prettier: `./node_modules/.bin/prettier --check src/expressionDescriptor.ts test/cronstrue.ts` reports a warning on test/cronstrue.ts. The same warning is on the unmodified HEAD~1 file (I checked a copy of it: `[warn] test/_b.ts`). Not caused by this patch.

## Who is affected (side-effect rule)

1. Anyone whose cron expression has a comma list in day-of-month, month, day-of-week or year with a plain first member and a later member with a step (`/`). Their output changes from malformed to well formed. Likelihood: low (rare expression shape). Anyone who parses or snapshot-tests the old text sees a change, but the old text had a glued time and " , ", so depending on it is very unlikely. Acceptable.
2. Users of 38 locales on the same expressions: same change; I saw no previously correct output changed. In vi and sw the step phrase's own leading words now survive ("mỗi", "kila"). That is a visible wording change in two locales, but it restores text that was being deleted. Acceptable. vi still reads oddly for months ("mỗi ngày 2 tháng", every day 2 months): that is existing vi step text, not this patch.
3. Users whose first member is a plain range with a later step (`0 9 * * 1-3,*/2`): the stray " , " goes, the glued "AMMonday" stays. New "At 09:00 AMMonday through Wednesday and every 2 days of the week". Still wrong, less wrong. PR.md states this under "Not covered".
4. Users on ar and ja: no change, still malformed. PR.md does not mention it (finding 2).

## Findings

1. (Minor, text) PR.md says the output "changed only in day-of-month, month, day-of-week and year, and only when a later member has a step" and quotes 849,660 combinations. I did not re-run that sweep; my sample (above) agrees. Not a blocker. If the operator cannot reproduce the 849,660 count, drop the number and keep "I compared old and new output over a sample of list combinations in every locale". Do not state a count that was not rerun.
2. (Minor, text) PR.md should say that ar and ja are unaffected and stay malformed, because their separator is not ", ". Add after the vi/sw sentence: "In `ar` and `ja` the separator is not ', ', so their output is the same before and after and still has a stray separator; I did not change that."
3. (Minor, scope) The fix leaves `1-3,*/2` glued ("AMMonday"). The PR is honest about it. A maintainer may ask for it in the same PR. Not a compat problem, so I do not require it. The author should expect that question.
4. (Minor, process) Open draft PR #387 (known_open_bugs.md) touches `src/expressionDescriptor.ts`. I did not check for a textual conflict with that PR. The hunk is near the multiple-values loop, so a conflict is possible but cheap to resolve.
5. (Minor, process) CONTRIBUTING asks for an issue first for non-trivial changes. This is about 3 changed source lines plus one test, no locale files edited, but output text does change in 38 locales. I think it is small enough to go direct, consistent with POLICY.md's rule (source under 30 lines, no locale wording edited). It is a judgement call and I record it as such.

No finding requires changing the patch.

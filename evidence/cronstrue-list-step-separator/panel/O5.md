## Exact rewrites

PR.md (replace everything after the title; 12 lines):

```
**Title:** Fix separators in lists with a plain first value and a step

**Cron Expression:** `0 9 * * 5,0-4/2`

**Expected Output:** `At 09:00 AM, only on Friday and every 2 days of the week, Sunday through Thursday`

**Actual Output:** `At 09:00 AMonly on Friday and , every 2 days of the week, Sunday through Thursday`

The expression is valid: a standard 5-field expression whose day-of-week field is the list `5` (Friday), `0-4/2` (Sunday to Thursday). `0 0 12 1,15-20/2 * ?` and `0 9 * 1,3-9/2 *` show the same defect.

Cause: in the multiple-values loop in `src/expressionDescriptor.ts`, the leading ", " is stripped from every member, including the first, so the first member runs into the time. A later step member keeps its own ", " after "and". Now only members after the first are stripped. The step-first list `0 9 * * 0-4/2,5,6` (`test/cronstrue.ts:753`) is unchanged.

`README.md:17` says "- Supports all cron expression special characters including * / , - ? L W, #" and gives no wording for combined lists, so the expected text follows the output for that step-first list.

Not changed: a plain range first with a later step, `0 9 * * 1-3,*/2`, still prints `At 09:00 AMMonday through Wednesday and every 2 days of the week`.

Full suite: 406 passing (405 before, plus the new test). In `vi` and `sw` the step text starts with its own comma phrase, which used to be stripped with the comma. For example `0 0 9 * 5,*/2 * *` in `vi` changes from "...và  2 tháng" to "...và mỗi ngày 2 tháng". I did not judge that wording.

Found by Quality Playbook, an AI code-review tool, with Claude; I reviewed the change.
```
(Only keep the `vi` line if the author keeps the locale mention; I reproduced both strings above. The `vi` change is in fact "5,*/2" month field.)

# S6: SHIP (high)

Role: performance. Scope: cost on hot paths touched by the fix. Verdict is about performance only. I did not judge wording or scope.

## Blind verdict (written from the patch, PR.md and source before CONFIRMATION.md)
The change adds one `i > 0` test and one `startsWith(", ")` plus `substring(2)` on a short string in the multiple-values loop of `getSegmentDescription` (src/expressionDescriptor.ts:595-597 and :613 in the fixed clone). It adds no regex, no new loop and no new allocation beyond one `substring` in the case where the string starts with ", " (the same cost the old code already paid for the plain branch). Per-call cost is a few nanoseconds against about 100 us for a whole `toString`. No hot-path concern.

I did not use a statistical benchmark harness. Conclusion: no measurable regression; the change cannot plausibly matter.

## Findings
1. No performance finding. No regex, loop or allocation change that needs a change request.
2. Optional, not required: the two branches now repeat the same strip logic (595-597 and 612-614). That is a style point, not performance, and the "minimal change" rule argues for leaving it.
3. Side-effect rule (performance angle): no user pays more time or memory. Output changes only for lists where a later member has a step (PR.md says so, and the old output was glued text or " , "). Who is affected: users with such lists in day-of-month, month, day-of-week or year; likelihood low; the old text was visibly broken. The `vi` and `sw` wording change that PR.md discloses ("và  2 tháng" becomes "và mỗi ngày 2 tháng") is a locale-wording matter outside my charter.

## Side-effect rule
Who is affected: anyone whose list has a plain first member and a later step member in day-of-month, month, day-of-week or year, plus lists with 2 or more step members. What happens: the string changes from glued or stray-comma text ("AMonly", "and ,") to well-formed text. Likelihood of a user depending on the old text: very low; no test, comment or doc made it intentional (CONFIRMATION.md grep). Non-English: vi and sw text changes with new words (`mỗi ngày`), disclosed in PR.md. Not a drop reason.

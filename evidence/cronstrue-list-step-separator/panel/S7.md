2. PR.md, the vi/sw sentence gives a wrong mechanism: "the step text starts with its own comma phrase that was stripped with the comma before". Nothing in the list loop strips that phrase. The cause is `src/expressionDescriptor.ts:798` at c08a7d6 (in `transformVerbosity`): `description.replace(new RegExp(this.i18n.commaEveryDay(), "g"), "")`. In vi and sw `commaEveryDay()` is ", mỗi ngày" / ", kila siku" (`vi.ts:73-75`, `sw.ts:73-75`), the same text the step phrase starts with (`vi.ts:118-120` ", mỗi ngày %s tháng"; `sw.ts:76-78` ", kila siku %s ya wiki"). With the comma gone the phrase no longer matches, so it is no longer deleted when verbose is off. Replace the sentence with:
   `In vi and sw the step phrase begins with the same words as commaEveryDay() (", mỗi ngày", ", kila siku"), and expressionDescriptor.ts:798 deletes those words when verbose is off. The deletion needs the leading ", ", so the phrase now survives in a list: `0 0 9 * 5,*/2 * *` in vi went from "...và  2 tháng" to "...và mỗi ngày 2 tháng" (a standalone `*/2` month in vi still prints "2 tháng"). ## Side-effect rule
Behaviour changes for any user of a list with a plain or range member first and a stepped member later, in day-of-month, month, day-of-week or year. English and most locales: output goes from glued/stray-comma text ("AMonly", "and ,") to well-formed text. Nobody can rely on the old text. Likelihood of the expression: low (rare lists). Only vi and sw users see a new phrase ("mỗi ngày 2 tháng", "kila siku 2 ya wiki"). vi's month case is the one that reads as a wrong unit, replacing already broken text ("và  2 tháng"). I judge this acceptable and not a DROP reason, but the PR must describe the mechanism correctly (finding 2) so the maintainer can decide. Scope: a valid expression, 11-line change, one test, so within "small fix" limits; no issue-first needed.

## Side-effect rule
Who is affected: users with a comma list in day-of-month, month, day-of-week or year whose first member is plain and a later member has a step. Before: a glued "AMonly" and a stray " , ". After: a well-formed sentence. Nobody can depend on the old glued text, and I found no test that pins it. In `vi` and `sw` the step text used to lose its own comma phrase and now keeps it (`vi`, `0 0 9 * 5,*/2 * *`: before per PR "và  2 tháng", after "và mỗi ngày 2 tháng"). That is a visible change in a locale I cannot judge, but it looks like a repair. Likelihood is low: this shape is rare.

Side-effect rule: who is affected? Anyone whose expression has a list in day-of-month, month, day-of-week or year with a
plain first member and a later step member. Their text changes from a glued, comma-broken sentence to a well formed one.
No one relies on the old text (confirmer found no test or doc that does). Likelihood of any user hitting it: low. Likelihood
of harm: very low. Also changed for the same users: a step member in the middle or end of a list (`1,3,*/2`) loses its own
leading ", " (old "and , every 2 days", new "and every 2 days"). That is the intended repair.

## Findings

1. Sibling case still wrong (disclosed, optional). `0 9 * * 1-3,*/2` still prints
   "At 09:00 AMMonday through Wednesday and every 2 days of the week" (old: "...Wednesday and , every ..."). Same for
   `0 9 1-3,*/2 * *` ("At 09:00 AM1 through 3 and every 2 days in a month") and `0 9 * 1-3,*/2 *`. So the PR title
   ("a plain first value") is accurate, but a list starting with a plain range is still glued to the time.
   I tried a one-line extension in my copy, in `src/expressionDescriptor.ts` line 593:
   `if (isSegmentRangeWithoutIncrement && (i > 0 || !doesExpressionContainIncrement)) {`
   Result: suite still `406 passing`; `0 9 * * 1-3,*/2` becomes "At 09:00 AM, Monday through Wednesday and every 2 days of the week";
   `0 9 * * 1-3,5/2,6` and `0 9 * * 1-3,5-6,*/2` also read cleanly; `0 9 * * 1-3,5` unchanged. The operator can either
   keep the PR as is (PR.md already says this is not covered) or add this line plus one test
   (`0 9 * * 1-3,*/2` expecting "At 09:00 AM, Monday through Wednesday and every 2 days of the week"). I would keep it as is
   if BUG-018 is meant to go out as its own PR. Not required to ship.

2. Locale side effect is real but disclosed (no change needed). In `vi` the month step text is ", mỗi ngày %s tháng"
   (`src/i18n/locales/vi.ts:119`, "mỗi ngày" = "every day"). `src/expressionDescriptor.ts:801` removes `commaEveryDay()` globally,
   which used to delete it only when the comma was still attached. Now `0 0 9 * 1,*/3 * *` in `vi` prints
   "Vào 09:00, chỉ trong Tháng 1 và mỗi ngày 3 tháng" while the 3-member `0 0 9 * 1,*/3,5 * *` still prints
   "chỉ trong Tháng 1 3 tháng, và ...". The existing locale string is the cause, and this is locale wording. PR.md already
   says "I did not judge that wording." Leave as is. The PR's `vi` example matches my run.

3. Existing defects exposed or left in other locales (pre-existing, not caused by this patch, do not report):
   `bg`, `ru`, `tg` month list "...и  всеки 3 месеца" (double space) and `id` "setiap hari 2  dalam seminggu"
   (double space). Identical in old and new apart from the repaired comma.

4. PR.md claim I could not reproduce: "I compared old and new output over 849,660 list combinations in every locale".

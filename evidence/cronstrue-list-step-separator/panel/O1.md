## Side-effect rule

- Who is affected: anyone whose cron list has a plain (non-range, non-step) first member in day-of-month, month, day-of-week or year and a later member with a step, e.g. `5,*/2`. What happens: the text changes from garbled (`AMonly on Friday and , every 2 days`) to well formed. How likely: rare input, and the old output was clearly wrong, so nobody sensibly depends on it. Anyone matching on the old string in a test of their own would see a change; that is the same risk as any text fix and is acceptable.
- Locales: in vi and sw the dow step phrase starts with its own comma phrase, which the old code stripped along with the list comma. Verbatim, fix vs base for `0 9 * * 5,0-4/2`: vi `Vào 09:00, chỉ trên Thứ 6 và mỗi 2 ngày trong tuần, Chủ nhật đến Thứ 5` vs `Vào 09:00chỉ trên Thứ 6 và , mỗi 2 ngày trong tuần, ...`; sw `Kwa 09:00, kwa Ijumaa tu na kila siku 2 ya wiki, Jumapili hadi Alhamisi` vs `Kwa 09:00kwa Ijumaa tu na  2 ya wiki, ...` (the base dropped "kila siku"). The fix restores the dropped words and does not contradict the meaning. I do not judge the wording, as PR.md says. ru keeps a pre-existing double space (`только май и  каждые 2 месяца`) that the fix does not introduce. ja has no ", " so it is unchanged and still has no separator after the time in both (`次において実施09:00金曜日 ...`); pre-existing, out of scope.
- No change to lists without a step (0 changed rows in my run). No change to step-first lists.
- Verdict on this rule: passes; no one is hurt.

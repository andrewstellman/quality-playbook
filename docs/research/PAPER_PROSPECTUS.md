# Beyond AI Code Review: Requirements, Fitness for Use, and Verified Defect Discovery

**Andrew Stellman · Working prospectus · 27 September 2026**  
**Proposed venue:** IEEE Software, peer-reviewed feature article.  
**Status:** Study proposal; comparative effectiveness and novelty remain to be established.

**Problem and contribution.** Software can pass tests and appear structurally correct while failing to meet users’ needs. Quality Playbook (QPB) applies an explicit quality-engineering process to this problem: gather evidence of intended behavior, derive persistent requirements, examine implementation against those requirements, and verify findings through executable tests. The proposed article evaluates that process and explains how practitioners can apply it. Its central question is: **Does requirements-centered quality engineering detect verified behavioral defects that a conventional AI-assisted code review misses, under comparable conditions and resource budgets?**

**Fitness for use.** Issue discussions can reveal use cases and behavioral expectations absent from official documentation. Gson is the proposed opening case: Claude’s review of an April transcript reports that issue discussions established a class of null-handling contracts, from which QPB derived requirements that helped expose the duplicate-key/null defect. The issues reportedly did not disclose that defect. We will inspect the transcript and trace the connection; an agent’s retrospective explanation alone does not establish causation. User requests are evidence to assess, not automatically binding requirements. Source relevance, authority, version applicability, and faithful interpretation will be evaluated.

**Evidence from practice.** Catalog all identifiable QPB targets and runs, including failures and rejected findings. Link distinct defects to their sources, validation, and upstream disposition. The README records merged fixes in Gson and Linux’s virtio-pci and zram subsystems. Local evidence folders record two additional nvmet patches sent on 10 September, with kernel reproduction captures and reviews. Verify upstream status at the reporting cutoff. Distinguish source inspection, modeled-harness execution, and execution against the actual implementation; preserve unknown evidence levels. Report unique projects, defects at each evidence level, submissions, and merges separately. Pending patches remain pending.

**Comparative evaluation.** Claude reports an April GPT-5.4 comparison of code-only and documentation-enriched QPB runs, surviving in transcript form after the original artifacts were lost. Audit this as historical pilot evidence about documentation enrichment, not proof of superiority over conventional review. Then freeze a protocol and evaluate cases not used to tune QPB. Use paired runs on identical pre-fix commits, isolated sessions, matched models, tools, corpora, and budgets. If an old model is unavailable, rerun both arms. Preserve a credible review baseline; disclose Council models and iterations or label a single-model QPB variant explicitly. Repeat runs and adjudicate findings independently of method where feasible.

**Documentation experiment and measures.** Cross review approach with evidence availability: official documentation alone versus official documentation plus eligible issue discussions. Both approaches receive the same corpus in each condition. This tests whether issue evidence helps and whether QPB uses it more effectively. Keep fixes, target-defect disclosures, and prior QPB findings outside discovery inputs. Measure unique verified findings, precision among adjudicated claims, recall against the eligible known-defect set, overlap and exclusive discoveries, run variability, time, tokens, and human verification effort. Audit the evidence-to-requirement links as well as the final bugs. Separate historical rediscovery from prospective discovery; disclose possible model-training contamination.

**Deliverable and next step.** Produce a practical article supported by a reproducible evidence archive, a focused related-work comparison, and explicit limitations. Begin with the catalog and Gson evidence reconstruction, then use a small paired pilot to finalize the experimental protocol before larger runs. The paper’s conclusions will follow the results, including ties, misses, and negative findings.
